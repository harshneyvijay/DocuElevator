Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about DocuElevator

What types of documents can I convert with DocuElevator?

DocuElevator supports a wide range of document formats including PDF, Word (DOC, DOCX), PowerPoint (PPT, PPTX), Excel (XLS, XLSX), Images (JPG, PNG), and many more formats for seamless conversion.

How secure is my data with DocuElevator?

We take security seriously. All uploaded files are encrypted in transit and at rest. Our servers are protected with enterprise-grade security measures, and we never share your documents with third parties. Files are automatically deleted after processing according to your plan's retention period.

Can I customize the appearance of my converted documents?

Absolutely! DocuElevator offers extensive customization options. You can select themes, adjust colors, modify fonts, and more. Our AI will even suggest improvements to enhance your document's visual appeal and readability.

How long does the conversion process take?

Most conversions are completed within seconds to a few minutes, depending on the document size and complexity. Larger documents or batch conversions may take slightly longer, but our system is optimized for speed without sacrificing quality.

Do I need to install any software to use DocuElevator?

No, DocuElevator is a cloud-based platform that works entirely in your browser. There's no need to install any software or plugins. This makes it accessible from any device with an internet connection, whether you're using a desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone.
